About the Academy

A campus that treats every stage of childhood as its own discipline.

Crescent Heights was founded in 1994 on a simple premise: a seven-year-old and a seventeen-year-old learn in entirely different ways, and a school should be built around that fact rather than one teaching style stretched across thirteen grades. Each division runs its own rhythm, its own facilities, and its own specialist faculty — while sharing a single campus culture of curiosity, discipline and care.

The Academic Pathway

Four stages, one continuous foundation.

01
AGES 3–5

Early Years

Play-based literacy, numeracy and motor-skill foundations in small, closely supervised groups.

02
AGES 6–11

Primary

Structured core subjects with hands-on science, creative arts, and the start of a second language.

03
AGES 12–16

Secondary

Matriculation and O-Level tracks, subject specialisation, and the first formal board examinations.

04
AGES 16–18

Intermediate

HSSC and A-Level pathways with university counselling and electives aligned to career goals.
